My role

My title was Marketing Analyst. The job was wider than the title: I was responsible for all the segmentation the business did.

I built and maintained the segment and interest code architecture behind CRM and lifecycle marketing, working in Adestra and the CRM, and with the BI team on reporting dashboards. It was the Excel work I had built at Informa that led to the role.

The repeat buyer model

Who keeps coming back, at three levels and three time windows

I engineered a multi-dimensional repeat buyer report that mapped 2, 3 and 5-year purchase histories at company, sector and portfolio level. It became the primary tool for identifying high-value recurring buyers and directing retention campaigns. Below is a working mock of how it read.

Frequency rules

Protecting the most senior audience from our own calendar

With 500+ products a year and three to four emails behind each, frequency needed one owner. Because I controlled every team's target audience, I centralised it for the most senior contacts.

2emails a day, at mostA hard cap for the C-suite audience, across every team's campaigns.
6 hrsminimum gap between sendsIf two emails did go out on the same day, they were never close together.
1test for relevance"If I were C-suite, would I do this?" Courses too junior for that audience were never marketed to them.
